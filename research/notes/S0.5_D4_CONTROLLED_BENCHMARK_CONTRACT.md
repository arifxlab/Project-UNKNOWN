# S0.5-D.4 — Controlled Benchmark Contract

**Project:** Project UNKNOWN — Autonomous Concept Formation & Model Discovery
**Stage:** S0.5-D.4
**Status:** Research specification complete
**Implementation status:** Not implemented

---

## 1. Purpose

S0.5-D.4 defines the controlled benchmark contract required to evaluate whether UNKNOWN can detect and respond to representation-level inadequacy without confusing it with parameter failure, model-family failure, stochastic variation, or insufficient evidence.

The benchmark must distinguish:

1. representation-sufficient conditions;
2. parameter failure;
3. model-family failure;
4. representation-level inadequacy;
5. non-identifiable / insufficient-evidence conditions;
6. stochastic/noise conditions;
7. unseen-context transfer.

The benchmark is an evaluation instrument, not itself evidence that UNKNOWN can discover representations.

---

## 2. Scientific Principle

Ground truth describes what is structurally true about the constructed environment.

UNKNOWN receives observations and experimental outcomes and must infer explanations from the permitted public interaction boundary.

Therefore:

> Ground truth and system diagnosis must remain separate.

Representation failure must not be defined as "the condition where a representation extension improves performance."

Performance improvement is evidence used to evaluate a structurally defined condition.

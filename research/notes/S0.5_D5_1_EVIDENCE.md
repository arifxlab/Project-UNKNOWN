# S0.5-D.5.1 — Evidence

**Project:** Project UNKNOWN — Autonomous Concept Formation & Model Discovery
**Stage:** S0.5-D.5.1
**Status:** Complete — research specification
**Implementation status:** Not implemented

---

## 1. Objective

Define an explicit baseline representation contract `R₀` that provides a stable scientific boundary for distinguishing representation-level inadequacy from parameter failure and model-family failure.

---

## 2. Research Decision

`R₀` is defined as a finite, typed, explicitly declared representation language:

`R₀ = (V₀, T₀, O₀, C₀)`

where:

- `V₀` is the primitive vocabulary;
- `T₀` is the type system;
- `O₀` is the permitted operator set;
- `C₀` is the composition constraint set.

The baseline is intentionally bounded and is not equivalent to unrestricted computation over raw observations.

---

## 3. Why a Bounded Representation Language Is Required

An unrestricted computation space would make representation failure difficult or impossible to distinguish from insufficient search.

If arbitrary functions over all public observations were implicitly available, almost any abstraction could theoretically be reconstructed within the baseline.

Therefore the benchmark must explicitly declare:

- what primitives exist;
- what types they have;
- what operators are allowed;
- how those operators may compose.

This creates an evaluable representational boundary.

---

## 4. Public/Private Boundary

The baseline representation may use only information legitimately available through the authoritative public environment interface.

The representation must not contain:

- hidden world state;
- hidden mechanism identifiers;
- evaluator labels;
- condition identifiers;
- representation-sufficiency flags;
- model-family labels;
- recommended candidate representations;
- future observations;
- future outcomes.

UNKNOWN receives environmental evidence rather than evaluator interpretation.

---

## 5. Baseline Representation Components

The baseline contract defines typed categories including:

- entities;
- scalars;
- vectors;
- booleans;
- categorical values;
- observable relations;
- time;
- ordinary actions;
- interventions.

Entity identities must remain semantically neutral.

Explicitly observable relations may belong to the baseline.

Derived structures belong to the baseline only when their construction is explicitly permitted by the declared operator and composition contract.

---

## 6. Representation Operators

The baseline may contain a finite set of explicitly documented operators such as:

- comparison;
- arithmetic difference;
- bounded arithmetic transformation;
- distance;
- aggregation;
- selection;
- pairwise relation construction;
- bounded temporal difference.

Each operator requires:

1. input type;
2. output type;
3. semantics;
4. deterministic behavior where required;
5. composition constraints.

An unrestricted arbitrary-function operator is excluded.

---

## 7. Representation Sufficiency

A required structure `Q` is representation-sufficient under `R₀` when:

`Q ∈ Expressible(R₀)`

Expressibility is a property of the representation language, not of current learned parameters.

Failure of one model family to recover `Q` is therefore not sufficient evidence of representation failure.

---

## 8. Representation-Level Inadequacy

A controlled R1 condition requires:

`Q ∉ Expressible(R₀)`

while a valid extension can establish:

`Q ∈ Expressible(R₁)`

The benchmark must additionally establish that parameter optimization and permitted model-family alternatives cannot repair the limitation.

Performance improvement is evidence supporting the representation hypothesis, not the definition of representation failure.

---

## 9. Representation Extension

Extensions take the form:

`R₁ = Extend(R₀, c)`

where `c` is a candidate representational construct.

Candidate provenance will eventually include:

- candidate identifier;
- parent representation;
- primitive components;
- construction operation;
- generation context;
- supporting evidence;
- predictions;
- intervention predictions;
- falsification conditions;
- complexity characteristics.

---

## 10. Structural Novelty

Candidate novelty is structural rather than lexical.

A new name does not establish a new representation.

The benchmark must distinguish:

- renaming;
- reparameterization;
- exact reuse;
- structurally equivalent reconstruction;
- genuinely new composition.

Formal equivalence testing remains a later design task.

---

## 11. Scientific Controls

The baseline contract requires controls against:

- metadata leakage;
- identity leakage;
- hidden condition information;
- unrestricted computational shortcuts;
- predefined target abstractions;
- model-family confounding;
- parameter confounding.

The representation boundary must be justified independently of UNKNOWN's eventual performance.

---

## 12. Scientific Review

### Question

Can a finite typed representation language provide a stable boundary for distinguishing representation inadequacy from parameter and model-family failure?

### Result

**PASS, subject to later implementation validation.**

The design is scientifically usable provided that the language is:

- explicitly declared;
- finite;
- typed;
- independently justified;
- publicly grounded;
- free from unrestricted arbitrary-function composition.

The language must not be deliberately weakened to manufacture R1 cases and must not be made so expressive that every target abstraction is already constructible.

---

## 13. Remaining Implementation Questions

The following remain open for later D.5 stages:

- exact primitive definitions;
- exact operator signatures;
- composition-depth limits;
- bounded temporal history;
- serialization;
- formal expressibility checking;
- structural equivalence checking;
- extension complexity;
- candidate execution safety;
- benchmark-specific representation languages.

These are implementation/design questions, not blockers to the D.5.1 research decision.

---

## 14. Final Decision

**D.5.1 decision:** Adopt a finite, typed, explicitly declared baseline representation language for `R₀`.

**Implementation status:** Deferred.

**Next dependency:** Define concrete world families and construct controlled R0/P1/M1/R1/N1/X1 conditions against this representation contract.

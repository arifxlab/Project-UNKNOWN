## Key Scientific Decisions

1. R1 is structurally defined and is not defined by improvement from an extension.
2. Parameter and model-family recovery controls are mandatory.
3. UNKNOWN receives public evidence, not evaluator diagnosis.
4. Intervention outcomes are evidence rather than automatic proof of a representation.
5. Evaluation must contain structural novelty rather than only unseen random seeds.
6. Candidate novelty is structural/provenance-based rather than name-based.
7. Complexity must be measured separately from predictive performance.
8. Candidate evaluation must include falsification and uncertainty.
9. Evaluation must be frozen and versioned before final measurement.
10. Clean evaluation evidence requires protection against candidate, benchmark, and feedback contamination.
